We are happy to announce **IJCCI 2027**, the 19th International Joint Conference on Computational Intelligence, held 27–29 October 2027 in Porto, Portugal. Niki van Stein is program (co-)chair of three of its conferences: **ECTA**, **EXPLAINS** and **AGENTICS**.

IJCCI brings together researchers, engineers and practitioners in fuzzy, evolutionary and neural computation, complemented by the transversal areas of explainable AI and agentic and generative AI. Speakers who cannot travel may present remotely (hybrid support).

## Conferences within IJCCI 2027

- **[ECTA](https://ecta.scitevents.org/)**: 19th International Conference on Evolutionary Computation Theory and Applications. Program chair: Niki van Stein (LIACS, Leiden University).
- **[EXPLAINS](https://explains.scitevents.org/)**: 4th International Conference on Explainable AI for Neural and Symbolic Methods. Program co-chairs: Kurosh Madani (University of Paris-Est Créteil) and Niki van Stein (LIACS, Leiden University).
- **[AGENTICS](https://agentics.scitevents.org/)**: 3rd International Conference on Agentic and Generative Techniques in Intelligent Computational Systems. Program co-chairs: Kurosh Madani (University of Paris-Est Créteil) and Niki van Stein (LIACS, Leiden University).
- **NCTA**: 19th International Conference on Neural Computation Theory and Applications. Program chair: Kurosh Madani.
- **FCTA**: 19th International Conference on Fuzzy Computation Theory and Applications. Program chair: Pietro Ducange (University of Pisa).

Conference chair: Francesco Marcelloni (University of Pisa). IJCCI 2027 is held together with WEBIST, IC3K and IN4PL; registration to IJCCI gives free access to these conferences as a non-speaker.

### Publication

The proceedings will be published by Springer in a CCIS series book and submitted for indexing by Web of Science (Conference Proceedings Citation Index).
