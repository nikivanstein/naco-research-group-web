We are excited to announce the special session **Beyond a Performance Scalar: Evaluation Science for Evolutionary Computation** at EvoStar 2027 (EvoApps).

Official website: [https://www.evostar.org/2027/evoapps/beyond-a-performance-scalar-evaluation-science-for-evolutionary-computation/](https://www.evostar.org/2027/evoapps/beyond-a-performance-scalar-evaluation-science-for-evolutionary-computation/)

## Evaluation Science for Evolutionary Computation

This special session invites original research on evaluation methods that go beyond single summary numbers for evolutionary and nature-inspired algorithms, such as final best fitness, mean or median over runs, or ranking tables. It covers three levels of evidence:

- **Theory and mechanisms**: convergence, runtime, stability, population dynamics, exploration versus exploitation, diversity, and component contributions.
- **Benchmarks**: property- and distribution-aware evaluation across landscapes, budgets, constraints, noise, dynamic changes, and fidelity levels.
- **Applications**: evaluation that combines solution quality with feasibility, reliability, uncertainty, interpretability, cost, resource use, and decision relevance.

Papers whose main contribution is a new optimizer judged only by final-fitness tables are outside the scope of this session. We seek methodological work backed by empirical or theoretical validation.

### Topics of Interest

- Anytime, time-to-target, and convergence-profile analysis
- Behavioural, trajectory, and diversity measures
- Ablation, sensitivity, and mechanism attribution
- Uncertainty, effect sizes, statistical comparison, and ranking stability
- Distributional and multi-criteria alternatives to scalar aggregation
- Explainable performance analysis
- Robustness and generalization
- Evaluation of multimodal, constrained, dynamic, noisy, and multi-fidelity problems
- Cost- and decision-aware evaluation in real-world applications
- Evaluation of evolutionary machine learning, AutoML, automated algorithm design, and LLM-assisted evolutionary computation
- Frameworks linking theory, benchmarks, and practical outcomes
