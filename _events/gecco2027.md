We are happy to be part of **[GECCO 2027](https://gecco-2027.sigevo.org/)**, the Genetic and Evolutionary Computation Conference, held 12–16 July 2027 in Kraków, Poland. Members of the Natural Computing Cluster chair three of its tracks: **ECOM**, **L4EC** and **Hot Off the Press (HOP)**.

## Tracks we chair

### [ECOM: Evolutionary Combinatorial Optimization and Metaheuristics](https://gecco-2027.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=52)

Track chairs: **Niki van Stein** (LIACS, Leiden University) and Francisco Chicano (University of Malaga).

ECOM is the forum for research on metaheuristics for combinatorial optimization problems, such as job-shop scheduling, MAX-3SAT and the travelling salesperson problem. It welcomes work that improves our understanding of combinatorial problems or of the algorithms that solve them, for example through landscape analysis, Walsh decomposition or the analysis of algorithm behaviour, and especially work that turns such insights into more effective optimizers. Grey-box approaches are welcome, as are novel real-world problems that are clearly described, reproducible and come with benchmarks. All submissions must link to an anonymised repository with data or code.

Topics include:

- Representation techniques
- Neighbourhoods and efficient neighbourhood search
- Variation operators for stochastic search
- Search space and landscape analysis
- Comparisons between techniques, including exact methods
- Constraint-handling techniques
- Automated design of combinatorial optimization algorithms
- Characteristics of problems and problem instances

### [L4EC: Learning for Evolutionary Computation](https://gecco-2027.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=2671)

Track chairs: **Elena Raponi** (LIACS, Leiden University) and Mario Andrés Muñoz (The University of Melbourne).

L4EC covers research that uses machine learning, statistical techniques or learning concepts, including deep learning, large language models and reinforcement learning, to improve heuristics and algorithmic components in evolutionary computation. Work that applies evolutionary computation to machine-learning problems belongs in the Evolutionary Machine Learning (EML) track instead.

Topics include:

- Large language models for evolutionary computation
- Automated algorithm design, selection and configuration
- Learning to control or coordinate EC algorithms (parameter tuning and control, dynamic algorithm selection and configuration)
- Extracting knowledge from population dynamics, search trajectories or genotypes
- Surrogate-based and surrogate-assisted optimisation of expensive fitness functions, including multi-fidelity approaches
- Feature-based characterisation of problems, such as exploratory and fitness landscape analysis

### Hot Off the Press (HOP)

Track chair: **Anna V. Kononova** (LIACS, Leiden University).

The Hot Off the Press track gives authors of recent work in evolutionary computation that has already been published or accepted at another venue, such as a journal or another conference, the chance to present it to the GECCO audience. It is the one GECCO track that accepts work already published elsewhere, so it is a good way to bring strong results from journals and other communities to GECCO.
