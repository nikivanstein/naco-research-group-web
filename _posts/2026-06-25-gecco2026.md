**Leiden, The Netherlands** – We are thrilled to announce that the XAI group (part of the Natural Computing cluster) at LIACS, Leiden University has had **5 full papers and 1 workshop paper** accepted at [GECCO 2026](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/), the premier venue in evolutionary computation. Even more exciting: **two of our papers are nominated for the Best Paper Award**!

---

### 🏆 Best Paper Award Nominees

#### Assessing Reproducibility in Evolutionary Computation
**Authors**: _Francesca Da Ros, Tarik Začiragić, Aske Plaat, Thomas Bäck, Niki van Stein_

How reproducible is EC research, really? We studied 10 years of GECCO papers, built a structured reproducibility checklist, and developed **RECAP**: an LLM pipeline that automatically audits reproducibility of published work. Our findings reveal clear room for improvement in the field — but also show that automation can help address this challenge at scale.

#### Structural Bias in Continuous Multi-objective Optimization
**Authors**: _Jakub Kůdela, Niki van Stein, Thomas Bäck, Anna Kononova_

Optimization algorithms can be systematically biased towards certain regions of the search space — regardless of the objective function. We extend this concept of *structural bias* to the multi-objective setting for the first time, introducing a suite of synthetic test problems designed to isolate purely algorithmic bias.

### Full Papers

#### LLaMEA-BO: LLMs Automatically Generating Bayesian Optimization Algorithms
**Authors**: _Wenhu Li, Niki van Stein, Thomas Bäck, Elena Raponi_

Can LLMs design better optimization algorithms than humans? For Bayesian Optimization, the answer appears to be yes. LLaMEA-BO uses large language models to automatically generate full BO algorithms, outperforming state-of-the-art BO baselines on 19 out of 24 BBOB benchmark functions — establishing a new paradigm for automated algorithm design.

#### Block-Bench: A Framework for Controllable Discrete Optimization Benchmarking
**Authors**: _Furong Ye, Frank Neumann, Thomas Bäck, Niki van Stein_

Block-Bench is a new framework for controllable, transparent benchmarking of discrete optimization algorithms. It provides fine-grained insight into algorithm behavior beyond just objective values, enabling a clearer understanding of where and why algorithms succeed or fail.

#### Landscape-aware Automated Algorithm Design
**Authors**: _Haoran Yin, Shuaiqun (Daniel) Pan, Zhao Wei, Jian Cheng Wong, Yew Soon Ong, Anna Kononova, Thomas Bäck, Niki van Stein_

This work decouples algorithm discovery from expensive function evaluations by combining Gaussian Processes with LLMs, making automated algorithm design practical for real-world problems where evaluation budgets are limited.

### Workshop Paper

#### EvoHIIT: LLM-assisted Evolutionary Design of HIIT Workout Programs
**Authors**: _Johana Chen, Niki van Stein, Robert Cabri, Thomas Bäck, Anna Kononova_

Presented at the EvoSoft workshop, this paper explores LLM-assisted evolutionary design of High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) workout programs, studying how human preference selection shapes the search process in semantic spaces. Excellent Master's student work by Johana Chen!
