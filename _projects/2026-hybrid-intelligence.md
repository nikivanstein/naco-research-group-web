This project is part of the [Hybrid Intelligence Centre](https://www.hybrid-intelligence-centre.nl/), a Dutch research consortium on AI systems that work together with people rather than replace them. Within the centre, project 3.23, **"Causal insights": adaptive explanations for interpretable probabilistic models**, develops causality-aware explanations for probabilistic graphical models. A particular focus is on how violations of modelling assumptions affect causal discovery, causal inference, and the explanations derived from these models.

The project is carried out by PhD candidate [Debargha Ghosh](https://www.hybrid-intelligence-centre.nl/who-is-who/debargha-ghosh/) (Intelligent Systems, Utrecht University), supervised by Dr. Silja Renooij (Utrecht University) and Dr. Anna V. Kononova (Leiden University).
