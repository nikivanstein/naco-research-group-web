**Large AI models for a resilient high-tech industry.** Foundation for Industry (FIND) brings together 5 universities with 10 labs, 11 Dutch companies, ranging from start-ups to multinationals, and 2 knowledge institutes to pave the way for a new wave of AI-based automation that helps the Dutch industry strengthen and keep its international competitive advantage as a leading high-tech nation in the AI-era.

FIND brings the benefits of foundation models, i.e. large AI models, to industry-relevant applications and data types that are not yet well served, in domains such as health tech, smart industry, and mobility and mobile robotics. The programme will also release an open-source toolbox of pre-trained models and code, so that companies outside the consortium can use the technology too.

More about the programme, its partners and its people is on the [FIND website](https://foundation4industry.nl/).
